# Photon Lab

A virtual photonics laboratory — light-matter experiments under real physics
constraints (numerical Maxwell solvers), experimental material design, and AI
inverse design, on consumer hardware. A Marsh-Press-Co group project.

**The founding question** comes from a witness statement in our
[Disclosure](https://github.com/Marsh-Press-Co/disclosure) archive: a federal
agent's flashlight beam that "went from shining far into the distance to
stopping about 50 yards away on nothing in particular." Experiment 001 tests
that description against Maxwell's equations: absorber physics vs cloak
physics, same beam, side by side.

**The honest frame:** amateur astronomy, not NASA — real photons through a
small telescope. Our solvers integrate the real equations; our experiments are
idealized (2D first, small domains, approximate materials). We reproduce
genuine physics and run genuine design searches. We will not be shipping a
cloak. Fundamental limits are things we observe in our own data.

Start here: `HANDOFF.md` (kickoff runbook) · experiments live in
`experiments/NNN-slug/` with a notebook page each. $0 policy: open tools only.
