## 2. Does the accelerating `kappa_window` collapse bear on whether `graded_black_shell`'s r-scaling is doing something physically different at r=312?

**Yes — there is a real, materials-side candidate mechanism the document's
own Next item 1 does not name, and it traces directly to this seat's own
Iteration-7 finding on this identical construction.**

NOTES.md's own Next item 1 offers exactly two candidate explanations for
the accelerating collapse (~20.7×, then ~185×): (a) a genuine near-field
geometric effect — the fixed-cell window offset (`D_eff=77`, unscaled)
becomes an ever-shrinking *fraction* of `r_out`, driving `z/z_R∝1/r²`
deeper into the Fresnel near zone as r grows; or (b) a floor/dynamic-range
artifact at `kappa_window(312)=4.8×10⁻⁶`. Both are legitimate and neither
is this seat's charter to adjudicate alone. But a **third, materials-
physics candidate exists and is not named**: holding `τ_shell=24.0` fixed
while `R_CORE`/`R_COAT` scale by κ does **not** hold the coating's
**absolute optical thickness in wavelengths** fixed — it cannot, by
construction, hold both simultaneously. Directly re-verified from `geom_*`:
shell thickness = `R_COAT−R_CORE` = 48/96/192 cells = **2.4λ/4.8λ/9.6λ**
at cpl=20 — a **4× growth** in electrical thickness from r=78 to r=312,
with `sigma_max` correspondingly shrinking 4× (0.5→0.125) to hold the
line-integral `∫σdr` constant.

This is not a new observation from this cycle — it is **this seat's own
finding at T8's founding cycle** (Iteration 7, exp-030 Phase-2 critique,
quoted verbatim in `LOGBOOK.md`): *"Holding σ_max fixed while r_in/r_out
both scale by κ means shell thickness-in-wavelengths ALSO scales by κ
(2.4λ→4.8λ→9.6λ) — not 'the same coating on a bigger object,' a
progressively... [structurally different] absorber."* At Iteration 7 this
concern was about the *ambient* (Weber-contrast) channel; exp-105 is the
**first cycle to run the coherent point/region-intensity channel on the
identical geometry**, and it is exactly this channel — sensitive to
phase-resolved near-field structure, not an angle-averaged silhouette —
that a shift in the coating's own internal optical character (thin,
sharply-curved, high-peak-σ at r=78 vs. thick, near-planar-in-λ, low-
peak-σ at r=312, matched only in line-integral depth) would most plausibly
perturb. A graded lossy medium's local complex index `n(r)∝√(1−iσ(r)/ωε₀)`
and its effective radius-of-curvature-in-wavelengths both change
materially across this family even though `∫σdr` does not; matching total
optical depth does not guarantee matching near-field interference/
standing-wave structure when the medium's own physical extent, in
wavelengths, changes 4×. This is additive to, not a substitute for, the
geometric z/z_R hypothesis already named — both could be operating
together.

**This hypothesis is directly, cheaply testable with existing machinery,
and the document does not name the test.** `experiments/052-.../` already
built the **fixed-absolute-thickness** `graded_black_shell` variant this
seat itself proposed at Iteration 7 (48 cells at every r, `τ_shell`
shrinking as `1/κ` instead of held fixed) specifically as the control
article for this exact confound. Re-running exp-105's own `kappa_window`
bridge on that article at r=156/312 would discriminate the two
hypotheses cheaply: if the accelerating-collapse *shape* (a ~20×-then-
~185× two-step acceleration, not a smooth power law) persists on the
fixed-thickness article, that is evidence the effect is dominated by the
geometric window/z-z_R mechanism (materials-invariant); if it changes
materially, that implicates the coating's own growing electrical
thickness as a real contributor. Neither this document nor its own
R3-meta-rule-invoking Next item 1 names this specific, already-available
control — a real gap in the resolution-check scope this cycle correctly
flags the *need* for but does not fully specify.

## 3. The blanket UNOBTANIUM label elides a genuine bench-scale-vs-scaling-law distinction

Computed directly (`dx_m=30nm`, from the Phase-1 Appendix, unchanged):
absolute shell thickness = 48/96/192 cells × 30nm = **1.44µm / 2.88µm /
5.76µm**. This seat's own Iteration-7 finding characterized real CNT-
black/metamaterial ultra-absorbers as achieving R≲1% at **absolute
thicknesses in the µm–mm range, independent of substrate size** — the
figure this document's own §5/Idealizations cites by reference
("self-similar r=156/312 constructions are LARGER absolute idealized
coatings, not more realizable ones... real CNT-black realizability
follows the OPPOSITE, fixed-absolute-thickness scaling law"). That
sentence is correct about the **scaling law** (a coating whose required
thickness keeps growing linearly with object radius does not match how
real ultra-black coatings are built) — but it is not the same claim as
"this specific r=78/156/312 geometry, taken as a static object, is
physically unrealizable," and the document's blanket "UNOBTANIUM-WITH-
PARAMETERS at every r" phrasing does not distinguish the two. At its own
absolute bench scale, 1.44–5.76µm sits comfortably inside the µm–mm range
this program has already cited as achievable by real ultra-black
coatings — the tag is correctly earned by the *scaling behavior* (what
happens if κ keeps growing toward witness scale, where this seat's own
Iteration-7 figure — 0.31–0.92m — is unambiguously unobtainium), not by
any one of these three particular bench-scale geometries considered in
isolation. This is a precision gap, not a wrong conclusion: no claim in
this document is false, but a future reader citing "UNOBTANIUM-WITH-
PARAMETERS at every r" without this qualifier could over-read it as "even
the r=78/156/312 bench geometries themselves are unbuildable," which this
program's own established citation does not support.
