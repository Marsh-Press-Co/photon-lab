# ELECTROMAGNETISM — Phase 2 Critique, Panel Iteration 44 (exp-067 candidate)

## 1. Steel-man (≤150 words)

I independently re-derived the network from `front_surface_conduction_correction`'s own definition: `CF_bracket_B = 1 + R_cond/R_rear` where `R_cond = L/(k_solid·A)`, `R_rear = 1/(A·h_combined)`, `h_combined = k_air/L + 4εσT³`. Inserting a third series element gives `CF_new = 1 + (R_cond + R_contact/A)/R_rear = CF_bracket_B + R_contact·h_combined` — exactly the proposal's `Bi_contact` term, dimensionally sound (`m²K/W · W/m²K = 1`). The `R_contact→0` identity is exact by construction, not approximate. I also hand-checked five table rows (stress A/B, second anchor) against this formula and every one reproduces to stated precision. The counter-intuitive TD-4-more-sensitive claim checks out too: solving for the R_contact that drives each margin to its bar gives bench crossing at R_contact≈5.4×10⁻⁴ vs witness at ≈1.0×10⁻², confirming bench is ~19× more sensitive in R_contact-space — a genuine, correctly-derived, non-obvious result.

## 2. Sharpest attack (≤150 words)

The algebra is correct but the *topology* is wrong. `R_contact` (root bonded to substrate) and `R_rear` (rear face losing to quiescent air + radiation) describe **mutually exclusive boundary conditions at the same face** — a face is either covered by a substrate or exposed to free air, not both. Stacking them in series forces `CF_with_contact ≥ CF_bracket_B` for every R_contact ≥ 0, structurally forbidding the possibility that a good bond (small R_contact into an effectively infinite substrate heat sink) outperforms free-standing rear-air loss — which is physically plausible, not exotic. This is exactly the boundary-condition ambiguity exp-063 itself flagged for this identical root-bonded scenario and answered with a genuine two-sided bracket (front-colocated-loss=1 vs rear-only-loss=worst-case, NOTES.md §"now a BRACKET"). This proposal, addressing precisely that deferred case, offers only a one-sided, monotonically-worsening stack with no complementary "contact replaces R_rear" endpoint, and doesn't disclose that the chosen topology can only ever report degraded performance, for any R_contact including an excellent bond.

## 3. Verdict: **support-with-changes**

The series-insertion algebra is sound, dimensionally correct, correctly recovers bracket B at R_contact=0, and the falsifiable predictions (including the TD-4/TD-5 sensitivity-ordering claim) are independently verified correct. Sourcing caveats and the vdW-mechanism idealization are honestly disclosed. The gate design (stage 25, four gates) correctly mirrors stages 23/24's precedent. The defect is scope, not arithmetic: the proposal silently narrows exp-063's established bracket discipline to a single, worst-case-on-worst-case endpoint for exactly the scenario (root-bonded) that discipline was built to bound, without flagging the direction of the resulting bias.

**Change that would flip to plain support**: add a second `bonded_substrate_conduction_correction()` endpoint — e.g. `R_contact` **replacing** `R_rear` rather than stacking in series with it (or a parallel combination, `1/R_rear_eff = 1/R_rear + A/R_contact`) — computed and reported alongside the current series number at every test point, with the model_note stating explicitly that the series form is a one-sided worst-case bound, not a general bonded-substrate model.
