## 3. Realizability judgment — my own charter's core question

This is where I have to be hardest on my own prior work, because it's the one question this cycle exists to move and the one my charter owns.

**Verdict: the realizability question for R_contact remains genuinely UNANSWERED — not "plausible," not "unobtainium-with-parameters."** Three independent reasons, each sufficient on its own:

1. **Zero `measured_direct` values exist.** Both anchors are `analogy_proxy_diagnostic` by the cycle's own honest tagging — proxies for a *different* physical interface (inter-tube vdW junction; forest/TIM boundary), not measurements of an actual CNT-forest root bonded to a mounting substrate. An analogy-based proxy is a legitimate placeholder for a *sensitivity* study (which is what this cycle actually delivered) but cannot itself support a published/plausible/unobtainium tier call — that requires a number describing the interface actually being modeled.

2. **The primary anchor's own units-legitimacy flag is unresolved, and is architecturally serious, not cosmetic.** I checked this against exp-063's own committed record (query 10's companion figure, ≈12 pW/K per-junction conductance): the `4×10⁻⁸ m²K/W` figure most plausibly describes a nanoscale per-junction contact patch, not a macroscopically-averaged areal density across a real root/substrate footprint — the same reason bulk forest κ sits orders of magnitude below single-tube κ. Applying it directly at macroscopic scale, as this cycle's "primary anchor" framing invites, is optimistic by an unknown, possibly large factor. Red Team's own A7 finding (independently correct, I re-verified it against exp-063's query-2 text: "short forest... interfacial resistance ≈0.6–0.7 cm²K/W" is explicitly a forest-to-external-surface figure, architecturally the closer analogy) means the cycle's own "primary" anchor is arguably the *less* relevant of the two it carries — a genuinely unsettled ranking, correctly disclosed but not resolved.

3. **The cycle cannot yet say which formula a real number should even be plugged into.** EM's mandatory second endpoint (A1) is the substantively important result of this whole cycle, and it cuts against a tier verdict rather than toward one: at the proposal's own Stress-B point, the series model says the witness-scale margin is "nearly erased" (1.0047×) while the replace-rear model says it is "comfortably clear" (1.1737×) — over an order of magnitude apart in R_contact-critical space (0.010213 vs. 0.004291 m²K/W). Both are honestly labeled as idealized endpoints, neither as "the" physics. Until a real interface is characterized well enough to know which topology it resembles (a partially-sealed bond vs. an effectively-infinite heat-sink substrate), a sourced R_contact number alone would not settle whether TD-5's margin is at risk — the model itself hasn't converged, independent of the number.

**A concrete process gap that follows from this**: `REALIZABILITY_MEMO.md` — the canonical document where this exact MATERIALS-charter tier question gets recorded (Entry 2, `graded_black_shell`, is the precedent format) — was **not touched this cycle**. No R_contact entry exists there. Given the cycle's whole justification was "the only queued item that can *move* a number, not just relabel or disclose one," it is a real omission that the one document whose entire purpose is recording exactly that tier verdict was never updated — even to record "UNANSWERED, pending a real measurement," which is itself a defensible, citable finding. I flag this as something Iteration 45 (or a same-shift addendum) should close, not something this Phase-5 review can silently wave through just because it was my own cycle's oversight.
