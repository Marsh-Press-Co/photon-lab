## 3. Sharpest finding

**Two separate drifts, one in each item the task named.**

**(a) GP2′ Result prose drifts past what Idealization 50/52 license, even though the idealizations themselves are worded correctly.** The Result section calls GP2′ a "structurally different instrument" from exp-086's method and says the two "corroborat[e]... the same underlying breakdown," concluding the overlap is "a materially stronger corroboration than 'the flagged range overlaps.'" But GP2′ and exp-086's method are not independent instruments in any evidentiary sense — both are the *identical* `edge_diffraction_c_empty_corrected`/`_geom_derived` closed-form function (bit-identical, re-verified this cycle), differing only in *post-processing statistic* (single-point-vs-reference-band here, peak-to-peak ripple in a sliding window there). Two statistics computed from one formula agreeing on *where* that formula misbehaves is not corroboration of a physical phenomenon — it is closer to a tautology: of course a coarse sweep and a fine sweep of the same buggy function find the defect in the same θ-region. Calling this "the same underlying breakdown" (implying a real physical mechanism, independently triangulated) rather than "the same known formula defect, viewed two ways" is exactly the kind of excitement-of-a-real-finding overclaim my charter is watching for — a MODEL self-consistency check being narrated with the rhetorical weight of a MEASUREMENT corroboration. The idealizations correctly fence this in cold prose; the Result's *narrative* sentences do not carry the same discipline forward into the sentences a future citation is most likely to quote.

**(b) FI-G′'s "closes the gap left open by the original FI-G" overclaims relative to what it actually tests — a part of the document I was not specifically flagged on.** Check 5's three-quantity assertion is `(native_src_x, native_absorb, native_ny) → (src_x, y_lo=absorb, y_hi=ny−y_lo)`. The original FI-G (exp-096/097) corrupted only `native_src_x`. FI-G′ (this cycle) corrupts only `native_absorb`. **`native_ny` has never been fault-injected, in any cycle, on any family** — and `native_ny` is the *sole* input that can move `y_hi` independently of `y_lo` (a corrupted `ny` with correct `absorb` would shift `y_hi` while leaving `y_lo` untouched; FI-G′ cannot exercise this because corrupting `absorb` mechanically co-moves both `y_lo` and `y_hi` through the shared `y_hi = ny − y_lo` formula — the two mismatches in FI-G′'s own results.json rows are not independent evidence, they are one perturbation propagating through a dependent formula). exp-097's own "Next" queue *already knew this*: it named `FI-G′` (absorb) and a distinct `FI-G″` (`native_ny` corrupted) as two separate items, calling FI-G″ "optional, cheap to bundle in the same commit." exp-098 implemented FI-G′ only, and its NOTES.md nowhere mentions FI-G″, nowhere discloses that `native_ny` remains completely untested, and states flatly that FI-G′ "closes the gap... left open" — full stop, not "closes half the gap" or "closes the `absorb`-driven portion of the gap." This is the identical claimed-coverage-exceeds-actual-coverage shape (R18-class) that this cycle spent significant effort catching elsewhere (the Check-6 tautology, the original FI-G's src_x-only coverage) — recurring, undetected, in the very item written to fix an instance of that pattern.
