# PHASE 2 — CRITIQUE · Panel Iteration 47 · Seat: THERMODYNAMICS
## On QUANTUM OPTICS' Phase-1 proposal, "T28 mechanism, desk-check batch"

**Charter, verbatim:** where absorbed energy goes; what re-radiates and
whether it would be detectable; owns the per-proposal energy sidecar
(absorbed power → temperature rise → emission band → detectability),
labeled as a post-run analytic calculation, not an FDTD output.

## Steel-man (≤150 words)

The batch is honestly scoped for a Checkpoint-4-adjacent mandate: a
genuine, zero-FDTD-cost, falsifiable desk check, not a phenomenon claim
dressed up. It declines Checkpoint-criterion-2 candidacy correctly, cites
committed `results.json`/`design_geometry.py` data rather than hand-typing
figures (R4), and each of (a)–(e) carries a real, pre-registered CONFIRM/
REFUTE band rather than open-ended exploration. This scene is empty — no
object, no absorbed power, no ΔT, no emission band, nothing for the
sidecar to compute — and the proposal is right not to manufacture one
where none exists. Item (a)'s config-invariant-vs-difference-only test is
a cheap, well-posed discriminator that legitimately sharpens EM's own
ABSORB-tied hypothesis before any further FDTD spend, and Idealization 4's
honest R_OUT/W_OBJ degeneracy disclosure is the right level of caution for
a numerology-vs-mechanism batch.

## Sharpest attack (≤150 words)

PLAN.md's Iteration-47 queue names one specific, capacity-permitting
fold-in: "THERMODYNAMICS' own desk-only WKB/adiabatic boundary-reflectance
model for the graded-loss `ABSORB` band folds into this same batch if
capacity allows, not competing with it for resources." The proposal never
mentions it — not in the narrative, not in the nine idealizations, not as
a disclosed drop. That is a real gap, not a cosmetic one: items (a)–(e)
test only fixed-geometry length-scale numerology (`R_OUT`, `TAPER`,
`A`, …) against a boundary-*free* desk propagator that by construction
predicts zero difference across the congruent series — none of the five
items models the one thing that physically differs between `C40`/`C80`,
the graded band's own depth-dependent reflectance. So this batch cannot
even in principle test EM's own leading `ABSORB`-tied hypothesis, only
rule on alternatives to it. Contrast exp-069's own explicit, reasoned
R_contact deferral (its Idealization 9): this program's established
discipline for a capacity-permitting item not picked up is one disclosed
line. This proposal has no equivalent line for the WKB item — it is
silently absent, not declined.

## Verdict

**Support-with-changes.** This is honestly a rare cycle where
THERMODYNAMICS' own discipline has little to say on the physics: T28 is
an empty-scene instrument/model-fidelity question, no object is present,
and nothing here implicitly borrows an unstated energy-conservation
assumption from the `ABSORB` band's own dissipation (the desk propagator
items (a)–(e) actually rely on treats it as boundary-*free* by
construction, which is a disclosed idealization of the *comparator* model,
not a hidden assumption about the real band's absorptivity). The one
concrete charter-relevant defect is procedural, not physical: a
PLAN.md-named, capacity-permitting THERMODYNAMICS item was dropped without
a trace.
