As pure classical instrument diagnostics this is squarely inside the Expressibility contract's escape hatch (no mechanism proposed, so my charter isn't strictly triggered) and I'd wave it through on those grounds alone.

**Sharpest attack:** Prediction (ii) — "rises monotonically… at most one local reversal" — is read off ONE coherent |Ez|^2 snapshot at ONE fixed launch phase. A coherent near-field intensity profile necessarily carries incident/back-scattered standing-wave ripple; a single phase reference samples one arbitrary point in that beat pattern, not the underlying envelope. This is the exact T25/T26 hazard this program already paid to characterize (T26: "a SINGLE fixed-phase realization — nothing bounds other phases"), applied to a different channel. Pre-registering the 11 x-values (R5) prevents cherry-picking WHICH points get reported; it does nothing to prevent misreading fixed-phase ripple AS a physical trend. A reversal could be genuine "fringe-limited near-field null" evidence or could just be this snapshot's fringe — the proposal has no way to tell them apart.

**Parameter change that would flip my verdict:** Replace the single fixed-phase |Ez|^2 standoff series with a phase-averaged one — sample each of the 11 x-points at 3-4 phase references (or use time-averaged Poynting-flux intensity over one period) and report envelope + spread, so prediction (ii)'s monotonicity claim is tested against a genuine trend rather than one coherent realization's interference structure.
