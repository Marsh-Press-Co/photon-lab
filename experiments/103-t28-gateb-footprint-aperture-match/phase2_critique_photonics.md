#### PHOTONICS — verdict: **support-with-changes**

**Steel-man:**
The edge=80 fix repairs a real confound: Gate B's own source silently diverged from the R4-family taper Gate B is supposed to validate against, so rerunning Gate B with edge=80 isolates the standoff question from an aperture question that was never meant to be in play. Using phasors()-derived |Ez|^2 for kappa is not as mismatched to the original figure as the proposal itself frames it: `Sim.envelope()` in `fdtd2d.py` builds `beam_behind` from `sqrt(snap_a**2+snap_b**2)`, a two-snapshot quadrature amplitude — phase-independent, not a raw single-instant reading — so both `kappa_window` and `beam_behind` are steady-state amplitude-squared ratios of the same monochromatic field, a stronger apples-to-apples footing than the "genuinely different instrument" hedge claims. Reusing r_out=78 unrescaled and documenting the H_REGION rescale as a skipped no-op rather than silently applying it is the right discipline. The 2-call budget is clean and correctly asserted.

**Sharpest attack:**
The six window-spanning kappa_region points (x=357,375,395,415,435,456) sit 18-21 cells apart — almost exactly one wavelength at cpl=20 (20 cells/lambda). A coherent |Ez|^2 field downstream of a partially-reflective graded shell carries standing-wave/interference structure with lambda/2-scale (10-cell) periodicity from forward-transmitted/back-reflected beat alone, before any diffraction-fringe spacing is considered. Sampling at ~1-lambda pitch is sub-Nyquist for a lambda/2 feature: it can alias a real oscillatory ripple into an apparently smooth monotonic curve, or vice versa — exactly what Prediction (ii) claims to test. The same blindness sits inside kappa_window itself: a single scalar spatial mean over a 5lambda x 2lambda box cannot distinguish "smoothly filling shadow" from "several fringe periods averaged to a number." Neither the window extent nor the standoff grid was checked against the field's own fringe periodicity — an unexamined confound of the same species (a spacing choice never checked against the physics it's meant to resolve) that sank Gate B last cycle, reintroduced one level down.

**Parameter change that would flip my verdict:** Tighten the window-spanning standoff pitch to <=10 cells (lambda/2) across [357,457), or report kappa_window's internal spatial variance/min-max alongside its mean, so Prediction (ii)'s monotonicity claim isn't Nyquist-aliased against the field's own standing-wave periodicity.
