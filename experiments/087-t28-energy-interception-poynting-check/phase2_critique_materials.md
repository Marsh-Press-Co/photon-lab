P6 is the **first** time this chain is fed by a genuinely new, unvalidated
oblique real-absorbed-power reading (36–42°, article-loaded Poynting box)
rather than T9's established broadside anchor — two provisional inputs
compounding into one "PRIMARY, pre-registered, falsifiable" verdict. P6's
own fallback (falsified only by a ~2-OOM departure) is wide enough today
because 699× is the anchor, but this program's own record shows
material-identity corrections swinging thermal margins by up to ~780×
(Biot, Iteration 22) and ~116× (H_CONV fix, exp-057 itself) — not
negligible next to a 2-OOM bar. If P6 ever does flip, nothing here
pre-registers that the flip must first be checked against known
material-constant-swing magnitudes before being read as new oblique
physics — the exact commensurability discipline R9 exists to enforce,
applied here to a compounding-uncertainty gap rather than a unit
mismatch.

realizability content. The gap is narrow but real: P6 silently combines
an ASSUMED material identity with a first-of-its-kind oblique measurement
without naming that combination as its own idealization, or pre-committing
to how a flip would be triaged between "real oblique physics" and
